EasyDrop is a temporary QR-paired file-sharing product. Senders select files, receivers scan and approve, and bytes transfer directly between browsers.

**Why:** The user replaced the initial nearby-device information-sharing brief with a QR-only file-transfer brief and explicitly excluded nearby-device discovery.

**How to apply:** Do not add simulated device lists or information-sharing cards; keep QR pairing, receiver approval, and direct file downloads central.
