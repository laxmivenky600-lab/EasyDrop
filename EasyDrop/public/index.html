HOW IT WORKS

How EasyDrop Works

Three simple steps to share your files.

01 — Create

Select your files and generate a temporary QR code.

02 — Connect

Scan the QR code or enter the Session Code to connect your devices.

03 — Share

Approve the transfer, send or receive files, and download them.

Features

Everything you need for a simple file handoff.

QR-Based Sharing

Connect devices quickly using a temporary QR code.

Two-Way Transfer

Both connected devices can send and receive files.

No Cloud Storage

Files are transferred directly between browsers via WebRTC without being stored in permanent cloud storage.

No Account Required

Start sharing files without creating an account or signing up.

Secure Connection

Connect devices through a session-based process with receiver approval before a transfer begins.

Simple and Fast

Select files, connect your devices, and share with a straightforward transfer experience.

Security & Privacy

Your files, your permission, your connection.

Receiver Approval

Review and approve incoming file transfers before receiving files.

Encrypted WebRTC Connection

WebRTC DataChannels use DTLS encryption to protect data in transit between connected browsers.

Temporary Sessions

Sessions expire automatically, and session access is restricted to authorized participants.

No Permanent File Storage

File contents are not permanently stored on EasyDrop servers.
